Private Lessons

Teaching

One-on-one guitar lessons — in St. Louis or online. For players who want to actually get somewhere, not just fill an hour.

Berklee-trained, with a Master's degree in Jazz Studies and years of teaching alongside a career as a working guitarist and composer. Lessons are built around what you're actually trying to play — technique and theory in service of the music, not a fixed curriculum.

Every student gets access to Practice Lab, the practice tools I build myself — drones, fretboard visualizers, and structured sessions for the time between lessons.

  • In person St. Louis metro.
  • Online Anywhere, over video.
  • Rates 30 min $45 · 45 min $60 · 60 min $75. Same whether in person or online.
  • Packages Save on any 4-lesson package: 30 min $160 · 45 min $215 · 60 min $270.
  • Availability Weekdays, with evening slots Monday, Wednesday, and Thursday.
