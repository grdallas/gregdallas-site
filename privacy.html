Privacy Policy

This page covers the information collected through this site's contact form and through lesson-inquiry forms (including Facebook and Instagram lead ads) for Greg Dallas's teaching and performing business.

What I collect

When you submit a contact form or a lesson inquiry, I collect the information you provide — typically your name, email address, and phone number, along with anything you write in a message field.

How I use it

I use this information only to respond to your inquiry, schedule and communicate about lessons or bookings, and follow up if you've asked about lessons. I don't use it for anything beyond that.

Sharing

I don't sell, rent, or share your information with third parties. Contact and lead forms are processed through the platform you submitted them on (this website or Meta's lead ad tools) and stored privately by me.

Questions or removal requests

If you'd like your information removed or have any questions about this policy, email gregdallasmusic@gmail.com.
