C · Db · D · F
E · A · D · G A · D · G · B D · G · B · e
Root
Other chord tones

Low E string on the left, high e on the right. Each column climbs the neck, position by position; the open-position voicing is fixed to this key. Some voicings are extremely difficult or impossible — attempt at your own risk.
