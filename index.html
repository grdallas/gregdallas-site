About

A conversation with tradition

Berklee-trained and back home in St. Louis, Greg works across jazz, original composition, and teaching — currently pursuing a Master's in Jazz Studies.

His recent work explores the evolving relationship between art and artificial intelligence: not using it to generate content, but to think recursively and push a creative practice somewhere new.
