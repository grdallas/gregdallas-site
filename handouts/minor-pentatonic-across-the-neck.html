Fretboard Reference

Minor Pentatonic Across the Neck

Five overlapping shapes cover the whole neck. Learn them in order, left to right below, and there's nowhere on the fretboard this scale doesn't reach.

Root
Scale tone

Each box's last note on a string is the next box's first — that's how all five chain into one continuous climb up the neck.

How the Boxes Connect

On every string, the second note of a box is the first note of the next one. Follow one string through all five boxes and it never repeats a fret — it just keeps climbing. That shared note is where one shape hands off to the next.
